The four pillars of wellbeing

Health isn't one thing — it's the balance of these everyday foundations.

🏃

Movement

Aim for 150 minutes of moderate activity a week. Walking counts — consistency beats intensity.

🥗

Nutrition

Fill half your plate with vegetables and fruit, choose whole grains, and stay hydrated.

😴

Sleep

Adults need 7–9 hours. A steady schedule and a dark, cool room make the biggest difference.

🧘

Mind

Manage stress with breathing, connection, and time outdoors. Mental health is health.
