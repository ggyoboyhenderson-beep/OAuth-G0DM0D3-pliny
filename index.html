Daily habits

Ten tips that actually stick

Pick one this week. Master it. Then add another.

  1. Drink a glass of water when you wake

    Rehydrate before caffeine to kick-start your metabolism.

  2. Move for 10 minutes after meals

    A short walk blunts blood-sugar spikes and aids digestion.

  3. Aim for 7–9 hours of sleep

    Consistency matters more than the exact number — keep a schedule.

  4. Fill half your plate with vegetables

    The simplest rule for better nutrition without counting calories.

  5. Take breathing breaks

    Three slow breaths before a stressful task lowers your heart rate.

  6. Stand up every hour

    Break up long sitting to protect your back and circulation.

  7. Get morning sunlight

    Ten minutes outside helps set your sleep–wake rhythm.

  8. Limit screens before bed

    Dim the lights an hour before sleep for deeper rest.

  9. Cook one more meal at home

    You control the salt, sugar, and portions — small wins add up.

  10. Practice gratitude

    Noting one good thing daily is linked to better mood over time.
