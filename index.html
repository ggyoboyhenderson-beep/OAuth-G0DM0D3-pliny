15-minute starter routine

Your first full-body workout

Do this circuit 2–3 times a week. Complete one round of each move, rest a minute, then repeat 2–3 rounds. Move slowly and breathe.

  1. March in place — 1 min warm-up
  2. Chair sit-to-stands — 10 reps
  3. Wall or knee push-ups — 8 reps
  4. Glute bridges — 10 reps
  5. Bird-dog — 6 each side
  6. Knee plank — hold 20 sec
  7. Calf raises — 12 reps
Ready for more? Build a plan →

First-timer tips

  • 🔥 Warm up for a couple of minutes first.
  • 🐢 Start light. Fewer reps done well beats many done poorly.
  • 💧 Rest & hydrate between rounds.
  • 🛑 Stop if it hurts (a normal "working" burn is fine; sharp pain is not).
  • 📅 Rest days count — muscles grow while you recover.

Your privacy, in plain language

  • 🔒 Everything stays on your device. Your profile, journal, streaks, and reminders live in this browser's local storage — there is no server, no database, and no cloud copy.
  • 👤 No account, ever. Nothing to sign up for, no email collected, no password to leak.
  • 🚫 No selling, sharing, ads, or analytics. The app makes no tracking requests. The only outbound calls are ones you trigger yourself: opening a source link, or looking up a food barcode on Open Food Facts (which receives only the barcode number).
  • 📤 You own your data. Export it as CSV or a full backup file anytime, restore it anywhere, or clear it forever with the in-app Clear buttons — it's gone, because there was never another copy.
  • ⌚ The native companion app (if you build it) reads steps, workouts, calories, and heart rate from Apple Health / Health Connect solely to show them to you — never for advertising, never uploaded.
